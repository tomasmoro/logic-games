This policy explains what personal data the KortexGames mobile application (the “App”) processes, why, who we share it with, and what rights you have over it. It applies to both the Android and iOS versions of the App.

Three-line summary. You can use the entire App without an account and offline — in that mode your data never leaves your device. If you create an account, we store your email and progress so we can sync them across devices. The App shows ads, and ads collect data — unless you are under 13, in which case only non-personalized ads are served.

For that reason, the first time you open the App we ask for your year of birth on a neutral age screen (it does not suggest an answer and cannot be revisited to change it). Based on your answer, the App behaves differently:

3. What data we process

3.1 Guest mode (no account)

You can use every game feature without registering. In this mode, your progress, scores, statistics, streak, and settings are stored only in your device's local storage. They are not transmitted to our servers and we have no access to them. Uninstalling the App deletes them.

3.2 If you create an account (optional, 13+ only)

You can sign in with Google or with email and password. In that case we process:

  • Identification data: email address and a unique user identifier. If you use Google, we receive your email and Google account identifier; we do not receive your Google password.
  • Credentials: if you register with email and password, the password is stored hashed by our authentication provider. We never see it in plain text.
  • Progress and gameplay data: games played, scores, completion times, correct and incorrect answers, level, consecutive-day streak, unlocked achievements, and daily goal.
  • Preferences: sound, haptics, and language settings.

We do not ask for and do not need your real name, phone number, address, location, or access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

3.3 Advertising data

The free version of the App displays ads served by Google AdMob. To do so, the AdMob SDK may collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS) — never for users under 13.
  • Your IP address (from which an approximate country- or city-level location is derived).
  • Technical device information: model, operating system, language, App version.
  • Ad interactions: impressions, clicks, whether the ad was completed.

Google processes this data as an independent controller. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3.4 Comparative statistics

When you finish a game, the App may show how you compare with other players (“you are better than X% of players”). That calculation runs on our server over aggregated, anonymous data: you receive a single statistical number. Individual scores or identifiers belonging to other people are never shown to you and never leave the server.

5. Advertising and consent

In the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland: before the first ad is shown, the App presents a consent form managed with Google's User Messaging Platform, compliant with the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework. There you can accept or reject personalized ads and see the full vendor list. You can change your decision at any time under Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy options inside the App.

On iOS: the system will additionally ask for permission to track you across apps (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, the IDFA is not used and you will see non-personalized ads. You can change this under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Users under 13: consent is not requested because the App tags all ad requests as child-directed and caps ad content rating at “suitable for general audiences.” Only contextual, non-personalized ads are served.

Premium plan: if you purchase the Premium subscription, the App stops showing ads and stops loading the advertising SDK.

7. Where data is stored and international transfers

Our servers are hosted with Supabase in the us-east-1 (N. Virginia, United States) region.

Because that region is in the United States, all personal data of users located in the European Economic Area is transferred outside the EEA. The same applies to some of our providers (Google). Such transfers rely on the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission and, where applicable, on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

8. How long we keep data

  • Account and progress data: for as long as the account is active. If you request deletion, it is erased within 30 days.
  • Inactive accounts: if you do not sign in for 24 consecutive months, we delete the account and its associated data after notifying you by email.
  • Guest-mode data: stays on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall it. We never hold it.
  • Aggregated, anonymous data (used to compute percentiles): retained indefinitely, as it cannot identify you.
  • Support emails: 12 months after the enquiry is resolved.

9. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data: encryption in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest, passwords stored using hashing functions, and Row Level Security at the database layer, which guarantees at the engine level that each user can only read and write their own rows. No system is infallible, but we will notify you of any breach affecting your data within the applicable legal deadlines.
